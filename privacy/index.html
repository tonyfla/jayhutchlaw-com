Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026. This policy explains what information Jay Hutch Law collects through this website and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you submit a form on this site, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, telephone number, the description of your matter, and any files you choose to attach.

We also automatically record marketing attribution data associated with your visit — the page you arrived on, the website that referred you, and any campaign parameters in the link you followed. This tells us which of our efforts brought you here.

How we use it

We use the information you submit to evaluate and respond to your inquiry, to contact you about your matter, and to maintain our client intake records. Attribution data is used only in aggregate to understand how people find the firm.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are transmitted to Clio Grow, the client intake system used by the firm. Files you upload are stored on Cloudflare infrastructure. Both providers process this data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.

Consent to contact

By submitting a form and checking the consent box, you agree that Jay Hutch Law may contact you by email or telephone about your inquiry. You may withdraw that consent at any time by replying to any message from us or by emailing contact@jayhutchlaw.com.

Confidentiality

Please do not send confidential or sensitive information through this website. Until an attorney-client relationship has been established in writing, information you send is not protected by attorney-client privilege.

Your choices

You may request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask that it be corrected, or ask that it be deleted, by contacting our office at the address below. We will respond to reasonable requests as required by applicable law.
